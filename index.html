Glossary

Die
One chip on the wafer. 188 per wafer in this lab.
Lot
25 wafers that travel and are measured together.
FOUP
The sealed box a lot travels in.
CD (critical dimension)
The width of a printed or etched line, in nanometres.
Metrology
Measuring the product: thickness, CD, resistance. Sampled, not exhaustive.
FDC
Fault detection: sensors on the tool, compared against a golden trace.
SPC
Statistical process control: the chart with a centre line and ±3σ limits.
R2R / APC
Run-to-run control: adjust the next lot's recipe from the last lot's result.
WIP
Work in progress: lots queued in front of steps.
Cycle time
Wall-clock time from lot start to sort. ≈ WIP ÷ throughput.
PM
Preventive maintenance: cleaning or parts that reset a tool's wear.
Yield
Fraction of dies that pass test.
Bin
The test verdict for a die: bin 1 passes, the others record why it failed.
Chamber matching
Keeping twin chambers identical, and reporting every metric per chamber.
Twin
A second chamber on the same step (ETCH-03A / ETCH-03B).
Spec vs limit
Spec is what the customer needs; control limits are what the process normally does.
